Volt Blocks — Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Developer: OC Labs · Contact: oclabsapps.support@gmail.com

Volt Blocks is a block-fitting puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it short because the game collects very little.

No account, no sign-up

You do not create an account to play. The game gives you an automatic player name (for example, "player_k7x2"). You can change the name, pick an avatar from six drawn faces, and choose a country. None of this is required.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in your phone's local storage so it can remember your progress:

  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, theme)
  • your best score, your local top-5 scores, and your daily challenge results
  • your player name, avatar choice and country, if you set them

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this information from your phone.

Online leaderboards

When you finish a game while connected to the internet, the game sends your result to our leaderboard server so it can appear on the Global, My Country and Daily boards. It sends:

  • a player ID made from your phone's Android ID for this app. The Android ID is scrambled (one-way hashed) on your phone first, so the real Android ID is never sent. It is used only to keep one leaderboard entry per phone, even if you clear the game's data or reinstall it. It is not your advertising ID, is never combined with it, and is not linked to your Google account or your real name
  • your player nickname (an automatic one like "player_k7x2" unless you choose your own), your avatar choice and your country, if you set one
  • your score and that game's statistics (pieces placed, lines cleared, longest streak, specials used and how long the game lasted), which we use only to check that the score is genuine

Visible to other players: your nickname, avatar, country, your best score and your Daily Challenge scores. The player ID and the game statistics are never shown.

The leaderboard data is stored by our database provider, Supabase, and kept until you remove it. You can remove it at any time in Settings → Online scores → Remove me, which deletes your leaderboard entries immediately, or by emailing us. Please don't use your real name as a nickname if you'd rather not have it shown publicly.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads, served by Google AdMob. Ads appear as an optional rewarded video (to revive a run) and as an occasional full-screen ad between games. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or similar).

Children

Volt Blocks is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for teens.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising data that Google's ad software handles as described above, and Supabase storing the leaderboard for us, we do not share information with anyone.

Your choices and requests

Your leaderboard entries can be deleted in the game (Settings → Online scores → Remove me) or by emailing us. Everything else stays on your phone and is removed by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. For questions about this policy, or to request deletion, email oclabsapps.support@gmail.com.

Changes

If the game adds features that use more information, we will update this policy and the date above before those features go live.
