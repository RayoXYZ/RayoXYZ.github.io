Lotus Mahjong — Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · Developer: OC Labs · Contact: oclabsapps.support@gmail.com

Lotus Mahjong is a tile-matching puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it short because the game collects very little.

No sign-up

You do not create an account or give us an email address to play. The game gives you an automatic player name (for example, "Player4821"). You can change the name, pick an avatar and choose a country. None of this is required.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in your phone's local storage so it can remember your progress:

  • your level, coins, power-ups and Genius challenge progress
  • your settings (theme, sound, music, vibration)
  • whether you bought Remove Ads
  • your player name, avatar and country, if you set them

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this information from your phone.

Online leaderboards

When you clear a level while connected to the internet, the game sends your result to our leaderboard server so you can appear on the Level, Weekly and Genius boards, globally and for your country. To do this the game creates an anonymous player account on the server. It is identified only by a random ID created for this purpose. It is not your Android ID or advertising ID, and it is not linked to your Google account or your real name. The game sends:

  • that random player ID
  • your player name, avatar and country. The country starts as a guess from your phone's language and region settings, and you can change it
  • the level you cleared and your score for it, and the Genius challenges you cleared, with the time each result arrived. We use the timing only to check that results are genuine

Visible to other players: your player name, avatar, country, highest level, points this week and number of Genius challenges cleared. The player ID is never shown.

The leaderboard data is stored by our database provider, Supabase, and kept until you remove it. You can remove it at any time in Settings → Online leaderboards → Remove me. This immediately deletes your leaderboard entries and your anonymous player account. Your levels and coins stay on your phone. You can also ask us to remove it by email. Please don't use your real name as your player name if you'd rather not have it shown publicly.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads, served by Google AdMob. You can choose to watch rewarded videos for a revive, extra coins or a free power-up. After the first 20 levels, a full-screen ad may also appear between levels, unless you bought Remove Ads. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or similar).

Purchases

Remove Ads and coin packs are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase went through, so it can unlock what you bought. Your purchases are described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Lotus Mahjong is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share information with anyone except in these cases: Google's ad software handles advertising data as described above, Google Play processes purchases, and Supabase stores the leaderboard for us.

Your choices and requests

You can delete your leaderboard data in the game (Settings → Online leaderboards → Remove me) or by emailing us. Everything else stays on your phone and is removed by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. For questions about this policy, or to request deletion, email oclabsapps.support@gmail.com.

Changes

If the game adds features that use more information, we will update this policy and the date above before those features go live.
